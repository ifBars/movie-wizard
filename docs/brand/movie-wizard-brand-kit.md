# Movie Wizard Brand Kit

## Design Read

Movie Wizard is a personal movie discovery app for film lovers curating a Plex-worthy library. It should feel closer to a calm modern catalog than a dashboard. The reference point is MovieLens browsing behavior, not MovieLens visual styling copied directly.

## Brand Idea

Find the next film worth keeping.

The app acts like a quiet recommendation compass: rate what you watched, mark what belongs in Plex, and get a better shelf of candidates.

## Visual Principles

- Simple catalog-first browsing.
- Warm light canvas with a charcoal utility header.
- Orange as the brand/action accent, borrowed only in spirit from MovieLens.
- Blue only for rating stars and rating-specific affordances.
- Posters are the primary visual asset.
- Avoid dark HUD styling, glass panels, neon gradients, heavy shadows, nested cards, and fake metrics.
- Keep controls obvious, compact, and close to the movie they affect.

- Brand orange: `#f26a21`
- Rating blue: `#2aa8df`
- Positive green: `#4b8b5b`

## Typography

- Use `Inter` for the app UI, with `Segoe UI`, `Helvetica Neue`, `Arial`, sans-serif fallbacks.
- Headings are compact, lowercase-friendly, and medium weight.
- Movie titles should be clear before metadata.
- Avoid decorative serif and oversized display typography in the working app.

## Component Direction

- Header: charcoal bar, small orange wordmark, central search, compact right-side controls.
- Movie rows: section title, horizontal poster cards, simple scroll behavior.
- Movie cards: poster first, title, star rating, one or two direct actions.
- Taste panel: narrow utility surface with the current profile and local-data actions.
- Settings/export: quiet utility controls, not a modal-heavy dashboard.
